What we do

Modern dentistry, gentle care.

From routine cleanings to full-mouth restorations — with iTero digital scanning and our own in-house dental lab.

Crowns & Bridges

Zirconia crowns and bridges with exceptional strength and natural translucency, for both front and back teeth. iTero digital scanning captures ultra-precise 3D images — no messy impressions — and our state-of-the-art in-house lab means quicker turnaround and superior quality control.

Dentures, Partials & Repair

Custom full and partial dentures built for a secure fit and natural look — new sets, repairs and adjustments, all crafted in-house.

Checkups & Cleanings

Gentle, thorough cleanings and comprehensive exams with digital diagnostics — screening for cavities, gum disease and oral cancer early.

Meet our family

The people behind your smile.

Real people who know your name — not a corporate chain.

Dr. Wisam Al-Rawi

Dentist · 20+ Years of Experience

Specializing in general dentistry, dentures and orthodontics, Dr. Wisam earned his B.D.S. from the University of Baghdad in 2004, followed by two master's degrees in oral imaging and oral radiology, and a DDS from the University of Detroit Mercy in 2015. Known for his patient-centered approach, he explains treatment options clearly and prioritizes comfort. Since 2020 he has served as a Captain Dentist in the U.S. Army Reserves Dental Corps. Outside the clinic, he enjoys the outdoors and hiking with his wife, Nada.

Kenny Moon, L.D.

Licensed Denturist · 10+ Years in Dental

A Bates Technical College graduate right here in Tacoma, Kenny is dedicated to helping patients regain confidence through high-quality, personalized denture care — dentures that feel as good as they look. A passionate communicator who speaks multiple languages, he connects with patients from diverse backgrounds. Off the clock you'll find him fishing or caring for his beloved dogs.

Thuan

Operations Manager · 15+ Years in Dental

Thuan believes a great dental visit starts long before you sit in the chair. From coordinating schedules to answering your insurance questions, he works behind the scenes to create a warm, welcoming environment for every patient. Around town, you might spot him photographing local events or vlogging for his YouTube channel.

Diane

Dental Assistant · UW Graduate

A University of Washington graduate, Diane is committed to helping patients feel comfortable and confident in their care. With a strong focus on patient education, infection control and chairside support, she creates a positive, welcoming experience for every visit. Outside the clinic she enjoys weightlifting and reading.
